How it behaves on the day

Once, and only on the day
The opening is offered only inside a set window on launch day. Once a visitor has been through it, their browser remembers and never shows it again.
Nothing to break
The site is already loaded underneath — the animation is only a cover being taken away. It cannot show a blank page, and anyone can skip straight through it.
Rehearsable
Any of the seven can be played on demand before the day, on the real site, without affecting what visitors see.
Considerate by default
Visitors whose device is set to reduce motion go straight to the site and see none of this. The demos on this page always animate, so everyone can judge them.
