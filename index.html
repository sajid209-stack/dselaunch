The opening, with sound

Confetti goes up as the panels clear. Everything here is the real thing, running live — not a video — over a screenshot of the new homepage.

How it behaves on the day

Once, and only on the day
The opening is offered only inside a set window on launch day. Once a visitor has been through it, their browser remembers and never shows it again.
Nothing to break
The site is already loaded and painted underneath — the animation is only a cover being taken away, which is why it can open straight onto the homepage with nothing to wait for. It cannot show a blank page, and anyone can skip straight through it.
Sound is never forced
Nothing plays until a guest presses the cover, which is what browsers require before audio may start — so the site can never make a noise at someone unasked. The mute sits in the corner throughout, and the whole sequence works in silence.
Considerate by default
Visitors whose device is set to reduce motion go straight to the site and see none of this — no animation, no sound, no confetti. The demos on this page always animate, so everyone can judge them.
Prepared for Dhaka Stock Exchange Ltd. The launch date, the wording on screen, how long the hold takes and how fast the curtain runs are all settings — any of them can change up to the day.
